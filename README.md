## The Challenge
The objective was to deploy a straightforward, two-page career site consisting of a Job Search page and a Job Detail page. Because of the simple requirement, the client did not have any custom design mockups available and initially planned to leverage a completely bare-bones, out-of-the-box template.

My challenge was to take a highly restrictive, rigid template layout and completely transform its visual identity. I needed to ensure the final product didn't look like a generic template, but rather a beautifully tailored digital experience that seamlessly aligned with Vertex's corporate branding.

<br />

## My Approach & Implementation
Since there were no design mockups provided, I took on the responsibility of building them a design from scratch. This was a great opportunity to flex my design and creative muscles. I began by reviewing Vertex's corporate site and branding guidelines to understand their core aesthetics, color palettes, and typography.

Using Figma, I built out a simple design concept. The project was initially going to leverage just a very basic design and layout off of a standard template, which is traditionally very bare-bones. My goal was to respect the underlying structural constraints of that layout but drastically level up the custom styles to match Vertex's corporate branding. As always, during the development phase, I ensured the code was fully responsive across all devices, cross-browser compatible, and strictly adhered to web accessibility standards.

<br />

## Results & Impact
This was a fun project to work on because of the initial limitations. While it is a simple two-page site on paper, I am showcasing it on my portfolio because it perfectly demonstrates my ability to turn bare-bones technical requirements into something that is aesthetically beautiful, engaging, and professional. The final product provided Vertex with a highly functional, brand-accurate recruitment tool that offers candidates a beautiful and intuitive user experience from their very first click.
